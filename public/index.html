Every problem comes with its fix

A developer can act on it. A site owner can understand it. This is what one finding looks like.

Example finding, written for this page

Images must have alternate text

Critical

A product photo has no text description. A person using a screen reader hears the file name, or nothing at all, so they can't tell what is for sale. Search engines also have less to go on when they index the page.

How to fix it

  1. Find the image named in the report and decide what it shows or does.
  2. Add an alt attribute that says so in a short phrase, such as the product and its view.
  3. If the image is only decoration, use an empty alt attribute so screen readers skip it.

Suggested fix

<img src="/images/shirt-navy.jpg" width="400" height="500"
     alt="Navy cotton shirt, front view">

Why it matters for your business

Missing alt text fails WCAG 1.1.1, the first success criterion, and it is quick to fix. Fixing it also gives search engines a description of every product image.

Questions people ask

What does Incline check?

It runs axe-core, an open-source engine, in a real browser on the page you give it. axe-core covers many of the success criteria in WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2, plus some best practices. Each problem it reports is a confirmed failure, not a guess.

Does a clean scan mean my site is compliant?

No. An automated scan is a strong signal, not a legal guarantee. It can't judge whether alt text is meaningful, whether the page makes sense with a keyboard alone or whether captions are accurate. Those need a person to test.

Does the AI invent problems?

No. axe-core finds the problems. The AI is only asked to explain a problem that was already confirmed and to write the corrected code. If the AI step fails, you still get the real findings.

Do you keep my scans?

Not at the moment. A report is sent to your browser and isn't saved on our side. To write the explanations, the snippet of page code behind each problem is sent to an AI service.

Can it scan a page behind a login or a staging site?

Not yet. Incline scans pages that anyone on the internet can open. Pages behind a login, on a private network or on your own computer can't be reached.
